## AdjustRowBytes wrap — ANALYZED, RULED OUT (do not chase)
`ImageIOPixelConverter::AdjustRowBytes(data, bufSize, a, b, c, d)` @ `0x18889e10c`, called from `JP2ReadPlugin::decodeImageImp` @ `0x188aa6294` (conditional `c != d`, requires `d > c` and `c >= a*4`). 32-bit `mul w9,w5,w3` (d*b) + `(b-1)*d`, `(b-1)*c` wrap; check `(uint32)(d*b) <= bufSize`. Loop dst offset uses wrapped 32-bit `(b-1)*d` but steps by full 64-bit `d` → can go below `data` (OOB underflow) when `(b-1)*d ≥ 2^32`.
**BUT not exploitable:** caller allocates buffer with REAL 64-bit size: `umull x25, w8, w9` (height × max(d,ctx[0xec])) → `__ImageIO_Malloc(x25)` @ `0x188aa619c`; bufSize = same. So the wrap needs `dstRowBytes × height ≥ 2^32` = **≥4GB allocation** → jetsams the iPad before the OOB write. Preview caps alloc at ~268MB (m2 4MB decoded, m3 268MB refused). Many-component sources have `c > d` → AdjustRowBytes errors out (wrong direction). PNG previews decode; **JP2 previews do NOT decode** (show "jpeg 2000 image" placeholder). Hand-built empty codestreams fail decode before reaching AdjustRowBytes.

2. **EXR decoder CVE-2026-28990** (heap overflow) — separate AppleEXR lib, need to extract.

6. **ihdr-vs-SIZ JP2 header mismatch** — speculative.
7. **KTX2/ASTC** texture paths (`IIO_Reader_KTX2::validateHeader` has `__throw_length_error` guard).
